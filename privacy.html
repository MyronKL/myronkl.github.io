Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What Jarvis accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Jarvis can read and organise their Gmail (search, read, label, archive and save drafts) and read and change their Google Calendar. It only does this for the owner, on the owner's own computer.

Where the data goes

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Email and calendar content is processed on the owner's computer, and the parts needed to answer a request are sent to the AI service the owner uses to run Jarvis.
  • Jarvis does not sell, share or publish anyone's data, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Jarvis does not send email without the owner approving each message.

Google user data

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops Jarvis from reaching the account.
